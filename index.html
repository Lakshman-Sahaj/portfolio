01 / WHY I BUILT IT

Turning aircraft performance theory into a working model.

Aircraft performance equations are usually introduced one at a time: lift, drag, dynamic pressure, power required, and climb rate. I wanted to see what happened when those relationships were connected inside one working model.

I built the Aircraft Performance Calculator to turn those equations into reusable software. A flight condition enters the program, atmospheric properties are calculated, aerodynamic forces are resolved, and those results feed directly into performance calculations.
